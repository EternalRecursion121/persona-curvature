Sampling the space

Seventy-two directions spread near-uniformly over the unit sphere of the space spanned by the first three factor-chart basis vectors — Warmth, Competence, Timidity — each steered at α = +1.5 and generated blind on the same eight questions. Click any point to read what the model becomes there. The faint dots behind are the 134 trait words on the same sphere, coloured by the Big Five factor they were drawn for.

Every direction elsewhere in this study was chosen for a reason. These were not: a lattice covers the sphere whether or not anything interesting is there, which is the only way to find out whether a coherent persona along a chosen direction means anything.

What the sampling measures

At α = +1.5, 45 of the 72 sampled directions produce no looping at all; the worst loses 38% of its responses, a mean rate of 6.60% across the sphere, with a mean response of 1390 characters. Most directions through this space give intact output, so the coherence of the directions we chose is not by itself evidence of anything.

Over the 72 scored directions and all 2,556 pairs of them, angular distance and judged Big Five profile distance correlate at ρ = +0.68 (Spearman). Directions less than 30° apart differ by 0.95 on the profile; directions more than 120° apart differ by 2.92. Personality varies continuously with position: this is a map, not a list.

The three axes carry 26.2% of the total centred adapter variance (11.6%, 10.2%, 4.4%). Counting which scale the judge rated highest at each sampled point: Agreeableness 35, Conscientiousness 18, Intellect 14, Extraversion 5.

Against the principal-component sphere
