What this is, and the four things that will mislead you if you skip them

One hundred LoRA adapters were trained, one per Goldberg Big-Five marker adjective. Each adapter is a weight-space displacement dW. This page browses the principal directions of the cloud of 100 displacements — what each direction is, which traits load on it, and, for the first three principal components, what actually comes out of the model when you push the weights along it.

1. The noise floor. Two adapters trained on the same adjective with a different seed are not identical.

2. The 0.447 evaluative baseline

Tucker congruence with the general evaluative target (“Eval” = +1 for every desirable marker, −1 for every undesirable one) is 0.447 for a perfectly recovered pure Goldberg factor, by construction — Eval is the sum of the five targets, so each scores 20/√(20·100) on it automatically. A factor scoring 0.48 on Eval carries essentially no extra evaluative content. Every Eval number on this page is printed against that baseline; the bar charts draw it as a line.

3. No factor clears the congruence bars

The conventional reading of Tucker’s coefficient is |φ| > 0.85 “fair similarity” and |φ| > 0.95 “equivalent”. Best congruences here sit in the 0.70–0.83 band. Nothing clears 0.85. Nothing clears 0.95. All five Goldberg factors turn up as separate, identifiable factors — that is a structural recovery, not a quantitative equivalence claim.

4. Coherence and truncation are the same column

Generation is capped at 250 new tokens. The judge penalises a response cut off mid-sentence as incoherent. The unsteered base model is the most verbose condition and therefore the most truncated, so its coherence score is already depressed. A steered condition that is terser will look more coherent for that reason alone. Every coherence figure on this page carries its truncation fraction beside it. Do not read “steering improved coherence” off these numbers.

5. PC5 and beyond are not interpretable

PC5 explains 3.1% and PC6 2.3% of centred variance, against a reseed noise floor that puts roughly 17% of squared between-trait distance down to training seed alone. They are shown for completeness and flagged. Any FA factor whose best Goldberg congruence falls below 0.50 is flagged the same way.
