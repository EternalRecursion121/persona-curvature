The three before it, all on `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct` and all in the second week of August 2026, are kept because the write-up leans on two of their findings (weight magnitude is a bad proxy for behavioural content; the factor structure is in the training text) and because the wiki cites them. `CONTEXT.md` (2026-08-14) is the contemporaneous summary; the wiki's `pages/history/` has one page per experiment and per lesson.

Three findings from this period that the zoo write-up inherits:

1. **Weight-space magnitude is a bad proxy for behavioural content** (`CONTEXT.md` section 4). Projecting the update off the sycophancy direction, off an oracle direction, off a rank-8 subspace, or gating it per module all left sycophancy at 9.2 of 10 while provably holding the constraint; only a KL penalty to the base (1.93) worked. The trait was pervasive (about 10% of energy in every one of 252 modules) and removing it changed nothing.
2. **The register is more legible than the content in a gradient, and only while it is being acquired** (`CONTEXT.md` 3.5 and 3.6): register effect z=232 against planted-fact identity z=42; the effect decays to zero by the fourth training quartile. Monitoring is available, forensics is not.
3. **The between-trait geometry is in the training text before it is in the weights** (`sweep100/WRITEUP.md`, `sweep100/text_baseline.py`): the chosen-minus-rejected embedding difference reproduces the weight geometry at RSA 0.826, the chosen embeddings alone almost none of it. The zoo's `analyse_goldberg_only.py` re-runs that deciding test on the new model and trait set (R^2 0.938 from the contrast, below zero from the constitution) and `analysis/fa_text_contrast.json` factors the contrast itself into the same five factors.

Where the oracle-direction number in `CONTEXT.md` 3.3 was wrong and how, and the other early corrections, are recorded in `wiki/pages/history/method-lessons.md` and `wiki/pages/overview/superseded-claims.md`.

`personality_lora_zoo_experimental_plan.md` at the root is the 2026-08-24 plan for the zoo phase that followed; `qwen35/plan.json` is the ledger version of it.
