## [2026-09-09] ingest | column-space structure: what the seed floor is hiding

Samuel asked whether LoRA adapters trained for the same trait from different random initialisations share COLUMN space (the span of B, in output space) even though their deltas are near-orthogonal in the Frobenius inner product. They do, and the row space is exactly at the null, which settles the mechanism behind [[seed-floor]].

New code `qwen35/column_space_on_modal.py` (thin factorisation: QR of A^T, SVD of the small d_out x 64 matrix, so no d_out x d_in matrix is ever formed) and `qwen35/analyse_column_space.py`; index `qwen35/analysis/column_space.json`; per-pair matrices `qwen35/results/column_space_{stage1,stage2,crossstage}.npz` and the column-space Gram `results/column_space_gram_stage1.npz`. `zoo-colspace.service`, three CPU-only Modal jobs, 727 + 751 + 1057 s of function time, about 0.83 container-hours and about $1.7 at the meter's flat A100 rate; the meter budget was raised by exactly the $5 authorised, 2418.00 -> 2423.00.
