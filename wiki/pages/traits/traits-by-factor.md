# Traits by factor

Two groupings of the same 141 pages. The first is the label the trait arrived with: which Big Five axis Goldberg's marker list assigns it to, or `Lexicon` / `Alignment` / `Hole` for the traits that were not drawn from that list. The second is measured: which of the five factors recovered from the adapter geometry the trait loads on most strongly.
