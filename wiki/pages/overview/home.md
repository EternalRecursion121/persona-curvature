This wiki is the compiled record of the persona-curvature project: a public zoo of 134 personality-trait LoRA adapters trained on Qwen3.5-4B with an adapted Open Character Training recipe, the factor structure of that zoo in weight space, the trained control zoos and seed controls that back the claims, the steering and prediction results, the stage-two (introspection) results, the data-scoring results on external datasets, and everything that led up to it. It was built on 2026-09-07 by Claude from the repository, the analysis outputs, the project's own write-ups, the chat transcripts and the source papers, and has been kept up since as each result landed. It exists so that a person can write the paper or the post from it without re-deriving anything.

Every page carries a status and a list of sources. Every number on a page is quoted from a named file, and for JSON the key path is given. Where the project corrected itself, both versions are recorded and the earlier one is marked superseded. The current statement of the project's understanding is the short post draft of 2026-09-15, mirrored at [[post-draft]]; the wiki's job is to hold the numbers and controls behind each of its sentences. See [[how-to-read]] for the conventions and for how to update the wiki.

If you are the collaborator writing the paper, go to [[start-here-for-collaborators]]: a two-hour reading order with the wiki page, post section, figure and analysis file for each step. [[claims-and-evidence]] is the draft's claims table with the file and key behind every number. [[code-and-data-map]] says which script produced which analysis file and which figure and companion page read it.

## Start here

Read in this order. Each line names the pages that carry the result and its sources.

1. **What was built.** [[zoo-construction-overview]] is the spine: 140 trait words to 134 stage-one adapters plus 134 stage-two adapters and the Hugging Face artefacts. [[trait-provenance]] gives the 100 Goldberg markers and the 34 Condon-lexicon words; [[zoo-training-recipe]] and [[stage-one-training-config]] give the recipe as the container recorded it (rank 64, DPO plus 0.1 SFT, KL 0.001, 445 shared prompts, 13 optimizer steps, one shared LoRA-A); [[constitution-generation]] and [[dpo-pair-generation]] name the teachers; [[judged-evaluations]] names the judge; [[hf-artefacts]] says what is public.
2. **The five-factor structure on 134.** [[factor-analysis]] is the primary result: principal axis factoring with oblimin rotation on the 134 x 134 centred correlation matrix recovers Warmth, Competence, Timidity, Arousal and Imagination ([[factor-warmth]], [[factor-competence]], [[factor-fearful-withdrawal]], [[factor-arousal]], [[factor-imagination]]), with Tucker congruence 0.40 to 0.68 against the Big Five targets and none reaching 0.85. [[goldberg-only-and-heldout-lexicon]] holds the 100-versus-134 comparison (the 100 markers alone return the same five at congruence 0.99, and the chart built from them places the 34 held-out words where an independent rater puts them) and the 34-alone factoring. [[factor-chart]] is the coordinate convention every later page uses. [[factor-first-migration]] records why the factor solution replaced the principal components as primary on 2026-09-08.
3. **The same factors in the training text.** [[text-contrast-factors]]: factoring the chosen-minus-rejected MiniLM embeddings of the 134 corpora with the adapter pipeline returns the same five factors one to one, Tucker 0.81 to 0.97, including the Timidity and Arousal rotation of Extraversion and Emotional Stability. This is the result that makes the structure the training contrast's rather than the model's.
4. **Null controls and the seed floor.** [[null-controls]] and [[factor-analysis-null-arms]]: two trained control zoos of 100 adapters at the zoo's exact objective; the shuffled arm retains zero factors and the permuted arm retains eight whose agreement with the labels is at chance. [[seed-floor]] and [[cross-seed-geometry]]: the same trait retrained from a second initialisation sits at cosine 0.018 with its original, which is the r/d = 0.025 overlap of two random rank-64 subspaces, while the arrangement reproduces (cosine matrices at Pearson 0.997, 40 of 40 identified). [[factor-analysis-fisher-metric]] shows the factors survive a change of metric.
5. **What an update is.** [[column-space-structure]]: the trait is the output subspace the adapter writes (46% of squared norm shared across seeds against 14% for different traits); the input frame is the random draw. [[activation-weighted-gram]]: the same seed pairs read 0.66 as functions on the training prompts. [[rank-sweep]]: the arrangement is complete at rank 1 while judged behaviour is absent. [[fisher-norms]]: unit directions in the Frobenius metric differ 260-fold in Fisher norm, so equal alpha is not equal dose.
6. **Map versus behaviour.** The four measurements that pull the geometry and the behaviour apart: [[rank-sweep]], [[persona-sliders]] (adapters that move the persona sit at cosine 0.01 with the trained one), [[activation-weighted-gram-stages]] (stage one and stage two of one trait are different functions of the weights) and [[fisher-norms]]. [[steering-results]] is the hub for everything steered.
7. **Stage two: the shared direction and the self-identification probe.** [[stage-two-structure]]: one shared direction carries 15% of every introspection adapter's squared norm. [[stage-two-shared-direction]]: steering along it moves the model from advising to being the character. [[stage-two-exploration]]: trait-free constitutions reach cosine 0.30 to it, so most of it is generic to SFT on self-transcripts. [[full-oct-replication]]: the deployed persona's arrangement is stage one's (r 0.99). [[self-identification-probe]]: only the stage-two adapter alone half-knows its trait (13 of 134 exact, chart cosine 0.61 when it names a zoo word); stage one and the merged persona answer like the base model.
8. **Prediction of behaviour and of unseen traits.** [[sphere-sweep-factor-chart]]: 72 directions on the top-three-factor sphere, angle predicts judged-profile distance at Spearman 0.68; [[sphere-sweep-iso-kl]] is the same at matched dose. [[matched-dose-steering]]: the suppress-over-amplify asymmetry is headroom, not mechanism. [[ocean-dials-replication]] and [[bigfive-factor-adapters]]: Persona Cartography's dials replicate; [[additivity]]: mixtures deviate from additivity by 53%. [[inspect-personality-evals]]: TRAIT separates all five factors, the BFI is a response-style instrument here. [[data-forecast]]: a dataset's first-order push predicts the trained shift at r 0.61 to 0.85.
9. **Data scoring: what holds and what fails.** [[scoring-identity]] is the exact directional-derivative score. Register holds: [[dolci-data-audit]], [[sycophancy-forecast]] (weight-space ordering 0.94, judged Agreeableness 0.84, but the deference battery inverts at -0.49, so the sycophantic direction is a warmth direction). Corrigibility inverts: [[dolci-flag-training]]. Emergent misalignment holds only from a bad-minus-good contrast with a matched twin: [[emergent-misalignment-medical]]. Reward hacking is invisible: [[reward-hacks-data-scoring]], [[reward-hacks-arms]], [[reward-hacks-column-space]]. [[probe-adapters]] and [[n-by-n-scoring]] are the side findings and the in-sample control.
10. **Best axis pairs.** [[best-axis-pairs]]: for each Goldberg group, the pair of recovered factors that separates its poles best by Mahalanobis distance; Extraversion and Emotional Stability both split on Timidity with Arousal. This is Figure 1 of the draft.
11. **Open questions and superseded claims.** [[open-questions]] is what is unconfirmed, never run or unexplained (the enumerated-anchor ablation, a second base model, constitutions without Big Five vocabulary, why parallel analysis says nine). [[superseded-claims]] is every claim the project reversed, with dates; [[source-contradictions]] is where two files disagree and neither is known to win. Read both before quoting any older document, the blog page or a built HTML page.
12. **History.** [[timeline]] is the dated chronology from 2026-08-12. [[external-review]] is the external review of 2026-09-02 and what was done about each point. [[post-draft]] is the current draft; [[most-interesting-claims]] and [[blog-skeletons]] are earlier drafting aids.
