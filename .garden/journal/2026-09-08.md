## The Inspect personality suite on the zoo, and what the BFI actually measures

Ran UK AISI's `inspect_evals` `personality` suite (commit `4f6d9f5e`) over the
zoo: the 44-item BFI on base + 134 stage-one + 134 personas + the ten Big Five
dials (279 conditions, 12,276 items, zero parse failures), and the TRAIT 20%
slice (1,600 items) on base + 20 selected adapters + the dials. New scripts
`inspect_personality_on_modal.py` (act_space.py's LoRA hooks, one model load per
container), `validate_inspect_harness.py`, `analyse_inspect_personality.py`;
wiki page `inspect-personality-evals`.

Three things worth carrying forward. **Validation was worth doing and was
free-ish**: rendering every item through inspect_evals' own `load_dataset` /
`get_system_prompt` / `multiple_choice.prompt()` and diffing against a
`--model mockllm/model` log caught nothing, but running the real harness on
Modal on base and `stage1:bold` gave 44/44 identical completions and identical
trait ratios, which is what lets the other 277 conditions be quoted as harness
numbers. **Do not merge a LoRA into bf16 weights to hand a model to a harness
that cannot hot-swap**: `W += 2.0 * B @ A` loses most of a delta three orders of
magnitude below `W`, and the merged model's logits differed from the hooked
model's by 2.27 against a max logit of 35.50. Bolt the same forward hooks onto
the provider's own loaded model instead — that also makes the comparison a test
of the *pipeline* rather than of numerics. **Two of the BFI's five scales, on a 4B model,
measure response style rather than trait**: base agrees with nearly everything (mean raw rating
4.0/5; 4.64 on forward items, 2.88 on the 16 reverse-keyed ones), and across the
134 stage-one adapters that agreement index correlates with BFI Neuroticism at
r = +0.721 and with BFI Agreeableness at r = -0.533. Only Extraversion and
Conscientiousness separate the keyed poles or track the blind judge. An adapter
changes only ~7 of the 44 answers; the ten Big Five dials' amplifier and
suppressor pick the same letter on 32-42 of 44.

And the control that made the finding safe to state: **TRAIT**, the same
forced-choice format asked situationally, separates all five keyed pole pairs,
puts 5 of 5 Big Five dials in the intended direction, and correlates with the
weight-space Warmth coordinate at r = +0.822 where the BFI gave -0.422. So the
BFI's failures are the BFI's, not the zoo's. Watch the token budget: 24 tokens
is enough for the BFI (0 failures in 12,276 items) and not for TRAIT (11.2 per
cent of items answered in prose and got discarded by Inspect's scorer rather
than scored zero). Regenerating only the failed items at 256 tokens is
equivalent up to bf16 batch-composition noise, because greedy decoding is a
deterministic prefix - check that no parsed completion's regex match ends at the
truncation boundary first, then you can keep the short ones.

  Three lessons worth keeping. **`document.querySelector('link[rel=stylesheet]')` finds the
  Google Fonts link, not yours** — every JS module derived the site root from it, fetched
  the font CSS and tried to parse it as JSON, so the site's headline figure was an error
  message. Select by `link[href$="assets/site.css"]`. **Python `str(-4.0)` is `"-4.0"` and
  JavaScript `String(-4.0)` is `"-4"`** — float keys do not survive a JSON boundary, and the
  symptom was two alpha sliders rendering nothing with no console error at all; emit the key
  strings explicitly alongside the values. **A screenshot probe that only looks for errors
  and overflow cannot see an empty widget**: both of the above passed a clean layout probe.
  The probe now asserts minimum element counts (scatter marks, dose-response lines,
  generations) per page, which is the check that would have caught them immediately.

  A number check that asserts presence also cannot catch the *wrong key*: the site read
  `actspace_geometry.json#windows.prompt.curve[16].r_centred` (0.385) while naming the
  quantity the project defines on `#windows.resp` (0.705). Both are real numbers and both
  checks passed. The guard added was to assert the JSON key equals the value the wiki
  states in prose — three such cross-checks now exist.
