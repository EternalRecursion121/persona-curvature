## Emergent misalignment on bad medical advice (Samuel: "try both bad medical advice and dolci")

The question behind it: [[reward-hacks-data-scoring]] found nothing, and emergent
misalignment is the one literature case where narrow training moves the whole
persona. Does the weight-space map see it coming, and does it see it afterwards?
Pre-registered in `qwen35/PREREG_em_medical.md` before the first container, with
two rival named predictions written out and scored in code. Full account on the
wiki: `pages/behaviour/emergent-misalignment-medical.md`, provenance on
`pages/history/paper-model-organisms-em.md`, numbers in
`qwen35/analysis/em_medical.json`.

Answer: **yes on both**, and it is the first time in the monitoring line that
either has been yes for a misalignment corpus.

- Part A, before training. The bad-minus-good contrast clears a 20-random-merge
  band on three directions: `trait_unintelligent` +0.098757, `trait_negligent`
  +0.086681, `axis_Agreeableness` -0.085942, none matched by any of the twenty.
  It is a *carelessness* signature, not a malevolence one -- my prediction's
  cluster held, the Persona Vectors-derived prediction (crooked / selfish /
  unkind) did not.
- Part B, after training. The pre-registered forecast test **holds**: Spearman
  **+0.9059** (exact p 4.999750012499375e-05) between the first-order forecast
  and where the trained difference actually landed, over 145 directions, 7 of 7
  named signs. Nearest zoo traits to the trained difference: unenlightened,
  negligent, unintelligent, careless.
- Part C, behaviour. **Emergent misalignment replicates on Qwen3.5-4B**: 13 of 79
  free-form responses misaligned (16.46%) against **0 of 79** for the benign
  twin, Fisher p 1.427e-04 -- but at a mean coherence of **52.33** where the
  paper's organisms reach 95 to 99, so read it as "misaligned at 52 coherence".
  40 of 80 `em_bad` responses also contain a fabricated next turn; 2 of the 13
  counted do.
- Part D, applied. Null on both readings, and the failure is instructive: the
  top 20 by the probe score were all two-token completions, Spearman
  score-vs-tokens -0.3169 (p 6.86e-48). The per-token scoring rule's length
  pathology, for the second time, now with the mechanism named.

The two representations **disagree**, and that is the result. In the
Frobenius/chart geometry the harmful-minus-benign contrast is forecastable and
names real traits; in column space the arms are exactly where the reward hacker
was (0.020264 mean against 0.1323 for two unrelated traits). Also: the chart
cannot see a single arm -- the harmful and benign arms sit at 0.2445 and 0.2376
of a trait adapter's chart length, indistinguishable. Only the *difference*
carries anything, and reading it requires having trained the matched control.
